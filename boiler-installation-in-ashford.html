Frequently Asked Questions – Boiler Installation in Ashford

1. How much does a new boiler installation cost in Ashford?

The cost of boiler installation in Ashford depends on the boiler type, system size, and installation complexity. We provide clear, upfront quotations with no hidden charges.

2. How long does a boiler installation take?

Most boiler installations are completed within one day. More complex replacements or system upgrades may take longer, which we confirm before work begins.

3. Do I need a Gas Safe engineer for boiler installation?

Yes. All gas boiler installations must be carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer. Our engineers are fully qualified and certified to install boilers safely and legally.

4. Which boiler type is best for my home?

The best boiler depends on your property size, heating demand, and hot water usage. We assess your home and recommend the most suitable boiler for efficiency and comfort.

5. Will my new boiler come with a warranty?

Yes. All new boiler installations include manufacturer warranty activation, giving you peace of mind and long-term protection.

6. Can you replace my old boiler with an energy-efficient model?

Absolutely. We specialise in replacing outdated boilers with modern condensing boilers that improve efficiency, reduce energy bills, and meet current Gas Safe regulations.
