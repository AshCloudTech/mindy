Gas Boiler Repair & Servicing FAQ

1. What does a gas boiler service include?
A professional gas boiler service includes safety checks, cleaning of key components, performance testing, and inspection for gas leaks or faults. Regular servicing helps keep your boiler running efficiently, reduces the risk of breakdowns, and protects your home from carbon monoxide leaks.
2. How often should a gas boiler be serviced?
Your boiler should be serviced once a year by qualified Gas Safe engineers. Annual servicing improves efficiency, extends the lifespan of your boiler, and helps maintain manufacturer warranty requirements.
3. Do you provide gas boiler repair services?
Yes, we offer a full gas boiler repair service for all boiler makes and models. Our experienced central heating engineers diagnose faults quickly and carry out safe, reliable repairs using high-quality parts.
4. When should I call a central heating engineer for boiler repairs?
You should contact a central heating engineer if your boiler loses heating or hot water, makes unusual noises, leaks, shows error codes, or frequently breaks down. Early repair can prevent more serious and costly damage.
5. Are your engineers Gas Safe registered?
Absolutely. All work is carried out by fully certified Gas Safe engineers, ensuring every gas boiler service and gas boiler repair meets strict UK safety and compliance standards.
6. What should I do in a gas emergency?
If you suspect a gas leak or serious gas fault, turn off the gas supply immediately, ventilate the area, and contact the official gas emergency number. Once safe, our engineers can assist with follow-up inspection and repairs.
