Frequently Asked Questions – New Boiler Replacement in Kingston

1. How much does a new boiler replacement cost in Kingston?

New boiler replacement costs in Kingston depend on the boiler type, system upgrades required, and installation complexity. We provide clear, upfront quotations with no hidden charges.

2. When should I replace my old boiler?

If your boiler is over 10–15 years old, frequently breaking down, or inefficient, replacing it with a modern energy-efficient boiler can improve performance and reduce heating bills.

3. How long does a boiler replacement take?

Most boiler replacements are completed within one day. More complex system conversions may take longer, which we confirm before work begins.

4. Can you replace my boiler with a more energy-efficient model?

Yes. We specialise in replacing old boilers with modern condensing boilers that operate at over 90% efficiency and meet current Gas Safe regulations.

5.Will my new boiler replacement include a warranty?

Yes. All new boiler replacements include manufacturer warranty activation, giving you peace of mind and long-term protection.

6. Do I need a Gas Safe engineer for boiler replacement?

Yes. All gas boiler replacements must be carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer. Our certified engineers ensure safe, legal, and compliant installation.
