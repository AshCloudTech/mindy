Gas Boiler FAQ

1. What is included in a gas boiler service?
A professional gas boiler service includes a full safety inspection, cleaning of key components, performance testing, and checks for gas leaks or faults. Our gas and boiler service ensures your system is operating safely, efficiently, and in line with UK Gas Safe regulations.
2. How often should a gas boiler be serviced?
Your gas boiler should be serviced once a year. Annual boilers service helps prevent breakdowns, improves efficiency, extends the boiler’s lifespan, and keeps your manufacturer warranty valid.
3. How much does a gas boiler service cost in the UK?
The cost of a gas boiler service varies depending on the boiler type and condition, but we provide clear, upfront pricing with no hidden charges. Our services offer excellent value compared to standard British gas boiler service options, with a more personalised approach.
4. Do I need to replace my gas boiler or repair it?
If your boiler is over 10–15 years old, breaks down frequently, or is inefficient, a gas boiler replacement may be more cost-effective long term. Minor issues can often be resolved with gas boilers repair, and our engineers will always give honest advice before recommending replacement.
5. Are new gas boiler installations energy efficient?
Yes. Modern condensing gas boilers achieve over 90% efficiency, helping homeowners reduce heating bills by up to 30% per year. A professional new gas boiler installation ensures your system is correctly sized and optimised for maximum efficiency.
6. Are your gas boiler engineers Gas Safe registered?
Absolutely. All our engineers are fully Gas Safe registered, qualified, and experienced in boiler gas installation, servicing, and repairs. Safety, compliance, and workmanship are guaranteed on every job.
